# Station Maintenance Procedure

> Fictional UrbanFlow operating procedure for Academy demonstration purposes.

## Intake and safety

Open a ticket for repeated disabled-bike counts, dock faults, power alarms, or customer reports.
Mark a station out of service when a safety hazard exists or when renting or returning is disabled.
Do not treat an out-of-service station as a rebalancing shortage.

## Repair workflow

Capture the station UUID, fault category, reported timestamp, technician, and source. Isolate unsafe
equipment, perform the documented repair, and run renting and returning functional checks.

## Closure evidence

A ticket may close only after a functional check passes, a completion photo is attached, and a UTC
completion timestamp is recorded. Synthetic maintenance records used by UrbanFlow are labelled
`synthetic` and are not presented as Citi Bike operational history.
